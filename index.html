01 // PHILOSOPHY

Why Ikigai?

We believe the best things aren't built simply because they can be built.

They're built because someone cares enough to make them exist.

Ikigai is the space where passion meets ability, purpose meets action, and ideas become meaningful. Our hackathon is an attempt to create that space.

「 Ikigai isn't something you find in 24 hours. It's something you begin discovering. 」

02 // THE CONTRAST

Not just another hackathon.

Most hackathons give you a problem and a deadline.

Ikigai gives you a space to explore.

We bring together curious people, meaningful problems and ambitious ideas to create an environment where building becomes more than competition. No slide deck theater. No disposable toys. A gathering of minds committed to building things that endure.
